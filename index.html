HADITH.

Privacy Policy

Effective 30 September 2026

HADITH. respects your privacy and is designed to provide a focused reading experience for authentic hadith.

Information stored on your device

HADITH. may store certain preferences locally on your device so the app can remember your experience.

  • Your selected reflection topics
  • Hadith you save as favourites
  • Your daily reminder preferences

These preferences are used to provide app functionality and personalise your experience.

Accounts

HADITH. does not require you to create an account to use the app.

Personal information

HADITH. does not sell your personal information.

If you contact support, information you voluntarily send, such as your email address and message, will be used only to respond to your request.

Notifications

If you enable Daily Reflection reminders, your device may schedule local notifications at the time you choose. Notification permission can be changed at any time through your device settings.

External services

HADITH. may open third-party services for features such as translation or scholarly commentary. Those services are separate from HADITH. and operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

Data retention and deletion

Preferences stored locally by HADITH. remain on your device unless they are changed, cleared, or the app is removed.

Support emails may be retained only as reasonably necessary to respond to support requests or maintain relevant support records.

Your choices

You can disable notification permissions through your device settings. You may also remove locally stored app data by deleting the app from your device.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if HADITH.'s features or privacy practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
